### The number that matters

**Nineteen stations are roadside or near-roadside (≤ 1 km). The app fires for fifteen.**

The distinction blurs in a city: at 727 m and 836 m, Jetoil and the Alexela at 106.2 km are a
90-second detour, not a different journey. Outside Tartu the bands are clean — a station is either at
50–200 m or it is 1.5 km away. Inside Tartu the whole notion of "on the route" softens, and the 200 m
radius starts making arbitrary-looking cuts through a continuous field of stations.

The two 200–500 m misses are the familiar **Alexela at 34.6 km / 266 m** — a near-miss on A, B and D
alike, never captured on any route — and an **Alexela at 101.0 km / 249 m**, in the middle of the Tartu
cluster.

## 2. The gaps — and the cluster

```
start ─29min─ ⚠️24.5 ✅24.7 ✅26.1 ─20min─ ✅42.5 ✅43.0 ─39min─ ✅75.9 ─27min─ ✅98.6 ✅100.1 ✅100.2

```

| leg | distance | at 50 km/h |
|---|---|---|
| start → Olerex/Alexela cluster | 24.5 km | 29 min |
| within that cluster | 0.2, 1.4 km | seconds |
| → Põltsamaa pair | 16.5 km | 20 min |
| within the pair | **0.5 km** | 1 min |
| → Laeva | 32.9 km | 39 min |
| → **the Tartu cluster** | 22.7 km | 27 min |
| within Tartu (98.6 → 108.3 km) | **9.7 km, ten stations** | **12 min** |
| → end | 11.4 km | 14 min |

**The last 10 km is the reason to drive this route.** Ten firing stations in twelve minutes, with gaps of
**0.1, 0.6, 0.7, 2.2, 0.8, 3.0 and 0.9 km** between them. Several are closer to each other than the
200 m radius itself.

That is the blocked-neighbour failure at maximum strength. On the Tallinn run, a pair 30 m apart
produced one clip and one loss; a pair 100 m apart produced one clip and a 4-second tail. Here there are
**three stations inside 700 m** (100.1, 100.2, 100.8 km) and another two within 200 m of the first pair.
With a single capture lane, a 120 s clip ceiling, and no queueing of blocked boards, most of this
cluster cannot be captured — and the ones that are will be a mixture of good clips and departure tails.

**Predict before driving:** of the ten Tartu stations, how many produce a clip longer than 10 seconds?
That number is the honest measure of the clustering problem, and this is the only route that can produce
it.

| **98.6 → 108.3** | **the main event.** Ten stations in 12 minutes. Count clips over 10 s versus tails under 5 s. Watch for a 120 s cap if the vehicle stops moving in traffic |
| 100.3 | the 4-price detection 240 m from Lõunakeskus — the one place a firing station and a real reading coincide |
| anywhere | releases at 250–290 m; beyond ~300 m means delayed location callbacks |

**A caution specific to this route.** Fifteen captures at roughly 90 MB each is about **1.4 GB of video**,
and Stage 1 is decoupled now so none of it will process during the drive — the queue simply grows. Check
free space before starting and drain the queue deliberately afterwards.
